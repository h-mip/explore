## Not medical advice

This website presents research results about mosquito bites. It does not give medical advice, and its maps and charts describe general patterns, not anyone's individual risk. For health information about mosquito-borne diseases, see [Canal Salut](https://canalsalut.gencat.cat) (Generalitat de Catalunya) or ask a health professional.

## About the results

The maps and charts show research estimates, which are uncertain. Each one comes with an explanation of its method and uncertainty. Results may be updated as new data and models become available. Content labelled provisional may change.
