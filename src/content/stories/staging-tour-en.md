This is a staging example, not a research publication. It shows how a reviewed story could combine short explanations, an image and an interactive view.

## Begin with the map

The map lets readers explore provisional monthly model estimates by H3 cell or municipality. A story can point to a particular question and place the interactive map alongside the explanation.

{{embed:en:map}}

## Add context around a visual

A published story could explain why a view matters, identify the dataset behind it and link readers to the [data and code page](/explore-staging/en/data/). Any interpretation of research results would need review before publication.

This demonstration contains no new findings. The time and activity pages elsewhere on staging use synthetic data solely to preview their layout and controls.
